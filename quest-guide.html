The survey fleet

Every player starts by choosing one airship. Another joins at every promotion, and any ship can be earned early by its deed. Each carries a survey instrument: 📡 Scan on the map rings the quakes it finds and flies to each.

Kinds of quake

A prediction logs every kind the quake belongs to (+ XP for each new one). In the game a lesson unlocks when its kind is logged; here they are all open.

The questions

Tapping a quake asks one question before its details appear. Which one is fixed for each quake, picked from the kinds that suit it. A wrong answer explains why, and the tutor can say more.

Daily missions

Three a day: up to two that need a matching quake on today's map, topped up with skill missions. Each done is + XP.
